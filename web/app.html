What does the wallet do here?

Every transaction is signed in your own wallet (MetaMask or any EIP-1193 wallet) on the GenLayer Studio network. This page never asks for, creates or stores a private key. Use a Studio test account, not one that holds real funds.

What the validators checked

PASS, FAIL and LATE come from plain HTTP facts every validator verifies itself. ERROR means a source could not be read (rate limit, outage); it is never counted as a failure of the work.
